# 1 · Brief

Write down five things before anything is designed. Use [`templates/BRIEF.md`](../templates/BRIEF.md).

1. **One message.** It must fit on a sticky note, for example "Problems get caught before customers notice."
2. **Where it plays.**
   - LinkedIn or X feed: 1:1 or 4:5, 30–60 s, silent autoplay, so it must work without sound.
   - Event booth screen: 16:9, a seamless loop, usually silent.
   - Website hero: 16:9 or 21:9, 8–20 s, a loop.
   - Launch keynote: 16:9 with music and voice, 60–120 s.
3. **Length.** Let the story set it. A four-beat story needs about 40–50 s. A logo sting needs 5 s.
4. **Sound.** Choose one: music-driven, voice-over, SFX only, or silent. "Silent" is a legitimate choice for booths.
5. **Truth source.** The product page or docs you'll take claims and numbers from, and nothing else.

**Gate:** you read `BRIEF.md` back and it describes the film you want.

**Anti-patterns**
- Listing every feature. Pick the one moment that proves the message.
- Starting with the logo. Open on the viewer's pain; the brand arrives at the drop.
