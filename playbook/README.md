# Playbook: seven stages, seven gates

| # | Stage | You decide | The agent produces |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Brief](01-brief.md) | one message, format, length, sound | `BRIEF.md` |
| 2 | [Components](02-components.md) | real UI or imaginary UI | a component inventory, captured tokens |
| 3 | [Theme](03-theme.md) | one theme out of 2–3 candidates | a theme sheet in your brand |
| 4 | [Storyboard](04-storyboard.md) | beats, order, on-screen words | `STORYBOARD.md` and a sketch page |
| 5 | [Build](05-build.md) | the timing notes | the HyperFrames composition and snapshots |
| 6 | [Sound](06-sound.md) | music, SFX, or deliberate silence | a beat grid and mixed audio |
| 7 | [Deliver](07-deliver.md) | final approval | 4K render, sized masters, loop check |

**Why gates?** A video looks "AI-made" when every decision is the first idea that came up. Two or three moments of human taste change that: which story, which theme, which frame to cut. Keep those decisions human and let the agent do the rest.

**Rules that apply at every stage**
- One message per film. If you need "and", make a second film.
- Show, don't caption. If the story only works while reading, redesign the frame.
- Never invent product capabilities, customers or numbers.
- Everything must be seek-safe and deterministic (see [05-build](05-build.md)).
