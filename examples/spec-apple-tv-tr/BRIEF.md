## What the film says

- **Message:** turn off the noise, and the story is what's left: Apple TV is now in Türkiye.
- **On-screen copy (Turkish, `lang="tr"`):** "Bu akşam ne izlesem?" · "Gürültüyü kapat." · "Apple TV" · "Artık Türkiye'de." · "Türkçe dublaj ve altyazı." · "iCloud+ aboneliğine dahil." · "Her ekranda."
- **Audience:** Turkish viewers who already pay for too many streaming apps and spend their evenings scrolling.

## Format

16:9 · 1920×1080 composition, rendered at 3840×2160 and delivered at 1080p · 24 s · 30 fps · sound: a score synthesised by `score.py` (no music licence needed) · no voice-over.

## Theme

[101 Noise Cancelling](../../docs/themes/101-noise-cancelling.html), redesigned for the living room: cinema black instead of pearl, white hairline waves, and an ice-blue quiet zone. The devices stay the same: the noise field, the quiet zone, a hero object lit like hardware (a generic screen slab, not an Apple device), two-tone headlines, and the cut to true silence.
